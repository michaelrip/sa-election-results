What do PR and all votes mean?

PR (proportional representation) is the ballot where you vote for a political party's share of your municipal council. The ward ballot chooses your ward councillor, who may be a party candidate or an independent.

All local ballots adds the valid ward ballots and municipal PR ballots so you can compare party vote totals across both local council ballots. It is a count of ballot papers, not unique people, turnout, council seats or a seat forecast. One voter can vote on both. Overstrand also has a separate district PR ballot for the Overberg district council; those votes are not mixed with municipal council votes.

About the data and sourcesHow to read the vote totals, 2021 comparisons and maps

2026 election votes: Preliminary Ward, municipal PR and Overstrand district PR results are imported from ESP’s IEC-derived public pages. This is an independent dashboard, not an official IEC declaration. The All local ballots view adds valid Ward + municipal PR ballot papers, not unique voters or a council-seat forecast.

2021 comparisons: Municipal Ward, PR and Ward + PR percentages come from official IEC historical CSV reports and are shown in brackets for the matching ballot type. Individual 2026-area ward comparisons use 2021 voting districts matched by The Ledger and may omit new or split districts. A party not listed historically is unknown, not zero.

Maps and reporting: The ward polygons are from the Municipal Demarcation Board, and suburb labels are descriptive geographical names. Counting districts is not the same as total ballots counted. Party leaders and ward-candidate results remain preliminary; final council seats depend on official allocation.
